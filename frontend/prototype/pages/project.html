A focused first version.

The scope comes from the team's final MS1 proposal. The model and infrastructure described here are planned work.

The experience

  • Two readers, five distinct favorites each, five new bridge books.
  • Fantasy and mystery, with a shared catalogue for both readers.
  • Separate reasons grounded in each reader's favorites and book metadata.
  • A 2D taste galaxy using the same book representations as the recommender.
  • Anonymous use with no accounts or stored names, emails, or reading histories.

The data plan

Use the UCSD Goodreads Book Graph's fantasy and mystery subsets. Reading histories support recommendation training; titles, authors, descriptions, and shelf tags support content features and explanations.

5,000distinct works at most
500ktraining interactions at most

These are MVP limits from the proposal. Retained counts will be reported after filtering. Editions are grouped by work before evaluation, and overlapping genre entries are deduplicated.

The data is for academic work and will not be redistributed through this website.

Does it work for both?

The proposal sets out how recommendations will be evaluated. No model or reader-study results are claimed by this demo.

Recommendation comparisons

Compare popularity, item-kNN, and ALS/BPR baselines with a hybrid recommender using reading histories and book metadata.

Compare average preference aggregation with least-misery aggregation, which uses the less-matched reader's score.

  • Min-Recall@10Measure the lower of the two readers' recall on held-out likes.
  • Ranking and coverageTrack mean NDCG@10 and long-tail coverage.

A small reader study

The study plan is for 12–20 volunteer pairs to review blinded recommendation lists. Measure joint acceptance, decision time, and explanation usefulness without collecting identifying information.

  • Shared acceptance targetBoth readers would read at least 60% of the recommendations.
  • Serving targetRecommendations in under 0.5 seconds per request, with a reproducible one-command rebuild.

These are proposal targets, not measured performance.
