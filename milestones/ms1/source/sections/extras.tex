\runin{Key attributes and relevance.} Interactions (\path{user_id}, \path{book_id}, \path{rating}, \path{is_read}) support collaborative training, while titles, authors, descriptions and shelf tags supply content features and explanation evidence; files join on \path{book_id}. For the MVP, each reader chooses five favorites from a catalogue of at most 5,000 distinct works, and training uses at most 500,000 historical interactions (actual retained counts reported after filtering).

\runin{Quality concerns.} We will remove cross-genre duplicates, group editions by \path{work_id} before evaluation, and flag missing descriptions and noisy shelf tags; older data and popularity bias may limit coverage, and since the files contain individual histories, simulated-pair scores cannot establish real joint acceptance. The dataset is limited to academic use, with no redistribution or commercial use.
